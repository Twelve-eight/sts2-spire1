[h1]Spire1: The Original Three Characters[/h1]

The three original Slay the Spire characters - Ironclad, Silent, and Defect - faithfully ported to Slay the Spire 2.

[hr]

[h2]Content[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]3 playable characters[/b]: Ironclad, Silent, and Defect, each with their original mechanic (Rage / Poison / Charge) intact
[*][b]233 cards[/b]: the full StS1 card pools (basic / common / uncommon / rare / status / curse)
[*][b]28 exclusive relics[/b]: Burning Blood, Ring of the Snake, Cracked Core and the other signature relics, numerically adapted to StS2 engine semantics
[*][b]53 StS1 events[/b]: every "?" room event from the original (act-specific events plus shrine-type shared events), routed into StS2's four regions following the original act mapping
[*][b]3 potions[/b]
[*][b]Full English localization[/b]: card / relic / event descriptions all support dynamic values
[/list]

[h2]Card pools[/h2]

Each character's cards join that character's own pool (they appear when you pick the character) and flow into the shared colorless / event-reward pools by the normal rules. Everything coexists with native StS2 content - no vanilla card is replaced.
